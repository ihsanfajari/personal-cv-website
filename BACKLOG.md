18. **Autumn Ridge rework — the first version is not good enough.** Playtest verdict 2026-08-20: the route is too simple, the corridor is far too wide, there are almost no obstacles, and the whole thing looks unpolished next to the CV island. Deferred, not abandoned. Concrete causes and fixes:
    - **Corridor width.** `CONTROL_POINTS` in `src/race/track.js` currently runs a half-width of 11–15, i.e. a **22–30 m wide** track for a 4.3 m truck — it drives like a six-lane road, which is why it feels simple. Target something like 7–10 half-width (14–20 m) on straights and 5–7 (10–14 m) in the technical parts, and let the width *vary* far more so the course breathes.
    - **No obstacles at all.** `src/race/props.js:131` skips every scatter candidate with `it.inside > 0.02`, so the corridor is swept perfectly clean by design. Needs a deliberate in-corridor obstacle pass: rocks and logs to dodge, a fallen tree narrowing the line, ruts/washboard sections, a rock garden, boulders on the apex of a corner. The collider grid and the knock-over system in `debris.js` already support all of it.
    - **Route shape.** 40 control points laid out as long smooth sweeps. Needs more event density: crests you cannot see over, a narrow ledge with a drop on one side, a hairpin that needs real braking (radius ~19 m is the floor — see the memory note), a split line where the short way is rougher, elevation used far more aggressively.
    - **Polish gap vs the island.** The island has glowing animated beacons, labels, an NPC with dialog, joke signs, achievements, edge markers, a quest compass, five biomes, sea/clouds/birds. The race world has a brown corridor and trees. Candidates: dust and mud spray particles, tyre tracks left behind, skid marks, a proper start-light gantry, spectators/marker flags, leaf litter blowing, distinct visual identity per sector, ghost of the personal best, sector split colouring (green/red vs best), engine and surface audio variation per section.
    - **Suggested order when it resumes:** narrow the corridor first (biggest single win, one array), then in-corridor obstacles, then route event density, then visual polish. Re-run `node scripts/track-report.mjs`, `scripts/race-smoke.mjs` and `scripts/race-wall-probe.mjs` after each step — narrowing the corridor will change lap time and may reopen the boundary questions.
