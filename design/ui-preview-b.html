i comparing the two options
Option A — Rally Roadbook (design-system.html): warm printed-paper world, hard shadows, stamps — strong personality, reads like an expedition journal, but its chrome visually competes with the 3D scene. Option B — Scenic Route (this page): borrows the game's own dusk palette and lowercase register, keeps the island as the hero, calmer and closer to the Art of Rally reference. Both share the same emoji-free icon sprite, so the choice is purely about voice: charmingly loud paper vs. quietly confident dusk.
