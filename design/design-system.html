Design System · v0.1

Rally Roadbook — a printed travel-poster UI for an off-road CV

A direction built to feel like a screen-printed rally roadbook and vintage national-park poster, not a generic AI-generated app. It keeps the site's existing paper/ink/rust palette but replaces every emoji with a drawn line-icon set, swaps soft glow shadows for hard offset "print" shadows, and adds paper grain, dashed route-stitching and rubber-stamp badges as the recurring motifs.

01

No emoji as UI

Emoji are the fastest tell of an unstyled, AI-default interface. Every icon here is a drawn 24×24 monoline symbol instead.

02

Hard shadows, not glow

Offset 1–2px "printed sticker" shadows replace soft blurred drop-shadows — flatter, more deliberate, less glassy-AI.

03

A typeface with a voice

Fraunces (display) + Work Sans (body) + IBM Plex Mono (data) instead of the default Inter/system-ui pairing.

04

Texture over gradient

Paper grain, dashed stitching and stamp marks stand in for the smooth purple gradients typical of generated UI.

Typography /foundations

Fraunces gives headlines a printed, editorial-poster warmth. Work Sans stays plain and legible for body copy. IBM Plex Mono is reserved for numbers and data — speed, odometer, coordinates — so readouts feel like a rally trip computer.
